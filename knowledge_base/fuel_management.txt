FUEL MANAGEMENT PROCEDURES

Document ID: FUE-001
Title: Fuel Monitoring and Management

1. FUEL MONITORING

Fuel quantities should be monitored regularly during vessel
operations.

The engineering team should maintain accurate fuel records.

Important fuel information may include:
- Fuel type
- Tank level
- Consumption
- Remaining quantity
- Transfer operations
- Bunkering information

2. FUEL CONSUMPTION

Fuel consumption should be monitored against expected operating
conditions.

Unexpected increases in fuel consumption should be investigated.

Possible causes may include changes in engine load, weather,
operating conditions or equipment problems.

3. FUEL TRANSFER

Fuel transfer operations should follow the vessel's approved fuel
transfer procedures.

Before transfer begins, relevant personnel should confirm the
transfer plan and communication arrangements.

Fuel transfer equipment should be checked before operation.

4. BUNKERING

Bunkering should be conducted according to the vessel's approved
bunkering procedure.

Before bunkering, responsible personnel should confirm:
- Correct fuel type
- Required quantity
- Tank capacity
- Communication arrangements
- Spill prevention measures
- Emergency shutdown arrangements

5. SPILL PREVENTION

Appropriate spill prevention equipment should be prepared before
fuel transfer.

Any fuel spill should be reported and handled according to the
vessel's pollution prevention and emergency procedures.

6. TANK LEVELS

Fuel tanks should not be filled beyond their approved safe capacity.

Tank levels should be monitored throughout fuel transfer operations.

7. FUEL QUALITY

Fuel quality information should be documented when required.

Fuel samples and relevant bunker documentation should be handled
according to vessel procedures.

8. LOW FUEL CONDITION

If available fuel becomes insufficient for planned operations, the
responsible officers should be informed.

The captain and engineering team should evaluate the remaining
fuel, expected consumption and voyage requirements.

9. FUEL RECORDS

Fuel transfers and consumption should be recorded accurately.

Records should be maintained according to applicable vessel
procedures and regulations.

10. FUEL MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLE

Fuel management should prioritize safe operation, accurate
monitoring, pollution prevention and reliable voyage planning.
