VESSEL MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES

Document ID: MNT-001
Title: Machinery and Equipment Maintenance

1. MAINTENANCE PLANNING

Vessel equipment should be maintained according to the approved
maintenance schedule.

Maintenance activities should be planned based on equipment
condition, operating requirements and manufacturer instructions.

2. PRE-MAINTENANCE SAFETY

Before maintenance begins, personnel should identify hazards
associated with the task.

Where required, machinery should be stopped, isolated and secured.

Energy sources should be controlled according to the vessel's
lockout and isolation procedures.

3. AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL

Only appropriately trained and authorized personnel should perform
specialized maintenance tasks.

Personnel should use appropriate PPE.

4. MACHINERY INSPECTION

Machinery should be inspected for signs of:
- Excessive vibration
- Unusual noise
- Leakage
- Overheating
- Abnormal pressure
- Abnormal temperature
- Damage

Abnormal conditions should be reported to the responsible engineer
or officer.

5. ENGINE ROOM MAINTENANCE

Engine-room maintenance should be coordinated with the responsible
engineering personnel.

Maintenance that affects propulsion, steering, electrical power or
other critical systems should be properly planned and communicated
to the bridge.

6. ELECTRICAL MAINTENANCE

Electrical maintenance should be performed only by appropriately
qualified personnel.

Electrical systems should be isolated when required.

7. TOOLS AND EQUIPMENT

Tools should be inspected before use.

Damaged tools should not be used.

Tools and spare parts should be stored safely after maintenance.

8. POST-MAINTENANCE CHECK

After maintenance, the responsible personnel should verify that the
equipment has been correctly reassembled.

Protective devices should be restored.

The equipment should be tested according to the approved procedure.

9. MAINTENANCE RECORDS

Completed maintenance activities should be documented.

Records may include:
- Equipment identification
- Date
- Work performed
- Parts replaced
- Personnel involved
- Test results
- Outstanding defects

10. DEFECT REPORTING

Equipment defects that could affect vessel safety should be reported
promptly.

Critical equipment should not be returned to service until it is safe
and authorized according to vessel procedures.

11. MAINTENANCE PRINCIPLE

Maintenance should preserve equipment reliability while protecting
personnel and vessel safety.
