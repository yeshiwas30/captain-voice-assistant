CREW OPERATIONS AND WATCHKEEPING

Document ID: CRW-001
Title: Crew Duties and Watchkeeping

1. CREW RESPONSIBILITIES

Crew members should understand their assigned duties and
responsibilities.

Personnel should follow instructions from authorized officers.

Safety responsibilities apply to all crew members regardless of rank.

2. WATCHKEEPING

Watchkeepers should remain alert and capable of performing their
assigned duties.

A person assigned to a safety-critical watch should not perform
activities that interfere with effective watchkeeping.

3. BRIDGE WATCH

The officer of the watch should monitor the vessel's navigation,
position, course, speed and surrounding traffic.

The officer should maintain an effective lookout.

Important navigation changes should be communicated to the captain
when required.

4. ENGINE ROOM WATCH

Engineering watchkeepers should monitor machinery and systems
according to their assigned responsibilities.

Abnormal temperatures, pressures, sounds, vibration or other
conditions should be reported.

5. WATCH HANDOVER

A proper handover should occur between outgoing and incoming
watchkeepers.

Important information should be communicated, including:
- Current vessel condition
- Machinery status
- Navigation condition
- Weather
- Ongoing operations
- Equipment defects
- Special instructions

6. FATIGUE

Crew members should follow applicable rest requirements.

Fatigue can reduce attention, decision-making and reaction time.

Personnel should report when fatigue may prevent them from safely
performing assigned duties.

7. DRILLS AND TRAINING

Crew members should participate in required safety drills and
training.

Drills help personnel understand emergency responsibilities and
communication procedures.

8. EMERGENCY DUTIES

Each crew member should understand their emergency station and
assigned responsibilities according to the vessel's muster list.

9. PERSONAL CONDUCT

Crew members should maintain professional behavior and follow
vessel safety requirements.

Unsafe behavior should be reported to the appropriate officer.

10. CREW COMMUNICATION

Crew members should communicate safety-critical information
promptly.

If an instruction is unclear, the crew member should request
clarification rather than make an unsafe assumption.

11. CREW OPERATIONS PRINCIPLE

Effective teamwork, communication, adequate rest and clear
responsibilities are essential for safe vessel operations.
