GENERAL MARITIME SAFETY PROCEDURES

Document ID: SAF-001
Title: General Vessel Safety Procedures

1. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

Crew members must use appropriate personal protective equipment
(PPE) when performing work onboard.

Typical PPE includes:
- Safety helmet
- Safety footwear
- Protective gloves
- Eye protection
- Hearing protection where required
- Appropriate protective clothing

The required PPE depends on the task and the hazards present.
