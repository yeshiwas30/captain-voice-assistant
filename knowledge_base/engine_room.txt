Engine Room Temperature Procedure

The normal engine room operating temperature should remain between
18°C and 35°C.

If the engine room temperature exceeds 35°C, the duty engineer
must investigate the cause.

If the temperature reaches 40°C, reduce engine load when operationally
safe and activate additional ventilation.

If the temperature reaches 45°C or higher, treat the situation as
an emergency and notify the chief engineer immediately.

All temperature incidents must be recorded in the engine room log.
